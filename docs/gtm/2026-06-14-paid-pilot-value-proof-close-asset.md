# Orvo Paid Pilot Value Proof Close Asset

Status: GTM execution asset
Date: 2026-06-14
Owner: Orvo N2 Pro GTM / Pricing / Packaging
Scope: Convert the first paid pilots by making value proof, risk reversal, and close language explicit while preserving Orvo's control-plane positioning.

## Executive recommendation

Keep the first paid pilot at **USD 149 / 30 days**, but rename the sales motion from “daily WhatsApp brief” to:

> **Orvo OS Activation Sprint — 30 días para validar el centro operativo de tu Tiendanube.**

The close should not sell more messages, more reports, or more AI. It should sell a bounded proof sprint:

1. one Tiendanube store as the operational control plane;
2. a named owner/operator who will act on cases;
3. evidence-backed cases for what needs attention today;
4. honest `data_stale` / degraded-data behavior when the source is not trustworthy;
5. a weekly value proof note that captures cases, manual checks replaced, false positives, and conversion signal.

The buyer-facing promise is:

> “En 30 días validamos si Orvo te sirve para reemplazar chequeos manuales y detectar antes lo que se está rompiendo entre Tiendanube, stock, envíos, WhatsApp y datos.”

## Why this close asset is needed now

Existing GTM docs already define the USD 149 pilot, OS Activation Sprint, onboarding checklist, ROI worksheet, and first lead segments. The missing sales asset is a tighter **value proof and risk reversal** layer:

- Buyers will ask: “¿Y si no aparece nada útil?”
- Price-sensitive merchants will compare Orvo to USD 7-30 Tiendanube apps instead of the total operational stack.
- Some prospects will want guarantees Orvo cannot make: revenue lift, autonomous actions, fiscal certainty, or perfect data.
- The first pilots must produce commercial evidence, not just product feedback.

This doc gives the sales motion a clear answer:

> Orvo does not guarantee revenue. Orvo guarantees a bounded operational proof process: useful cases, data-health findings, and a decision by day 25-28.

## Pricing and packaging decision

### Keep the current ladder

| Tier | Current recommendation | Buyer-facing meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Activation Sprint / Pilot | USD 149 / 30 days | Concierge setup, one Tiendanube store, daily brief, cases/evidence, data-health audit, weekly review. |
| Starter | USD 79-99 / mo | One-store daily operating queue after pilot. |
| Growth | USD 199 / mo | More workflow depth, more recipients/checks/history, and readiness-gated expansion such as fulfillment or Meta Ads when truth gates pass. |
| Custom | USD 399+ scoped | Multi-store, agency, custom connectors, formal SLA/security. |

### Do not discount the pilot casually

Use discounts only as a structured trade:

- **Clean Band A lead:** USD 149, no discount.
- **Strategic design partner:** USD 149 pilot; optionally credit USD 79-99 to the first paid month if they convert within 14 days.
- **Low-volume but high-learning store:** pilot only if the learning is exceptional.
- **Free pilot request:** decline; offer a short data-health/checklist conversation or nurture content.
- **Multi-store request:** do not bundle into Starter; route to Custom waitlist or one-store pilot only.

### No guarantee language to use

Avoid:

- “te recupero ventas garantizado”;
- “pausamos campañas automáticamente”;
- “reemplazamos tu ERP/facturación/envíos”;
- “siempre vas a tener casos todos los días”;
- “Orvo sabe si falta facturar” unless the fiscal source and permissions are verified.

Use:

- “validamos si detectamos casos útiles con evidencia”;
- “si el dato no es confiable, Orvo lo marca como stale”;
- “una persona decide; Orvo detecta y recomienda”;
- “WhatsApp te avisa; Orvo guarda el historial”;
- “al día 25-28 decidimos Starter, Growth, limpieza de datos o stop”.

## Risk reversal: the 30-day value proof promise

### Recommended buyer-facing promise

```text
Piloto Orvo OS Activation Sprint — USD 149 / 30 días

Durante 30 días, Orvo arma el centro operativo inicial de una tienda Tiendanube:
casos, evidencia, datos stale/no confiables, stock en riesgo y ventas bajo piso
configurado cuando aplique. WhatsApp te avisa; la cola y el historial quedan en Orvo.

Si durante el piloto Orvo no abre al menos 1 caso útil con evidencia, o no muestra
claramente un problema de datos que te ayude a decidir, no tiene sentido que sigas
pagando. Lo cerramos limpio: Starter, Growth, limpieza de datos, pausa o stop.
```

### What this guarantee does and does not cover

| Promise | Covered? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| At least one useful case with evidence | Yes | Core pilot value proof. |
| Clear data-health finding if source is unreliable | Yes | `data_stale` / degraded-data honesty is a value signal. |
| Daily WhatsApp brief delivered | Yes, if channel setup succeeds | This validates habit and projection surface. |
| Weekly review / value note | Yes | Needed for conversion decision. |
| Revenue increase | No | Would be an unsupported guarantee. |
| Stockout avoided | Only if merchant action and evidence support it | Avoid inventing counterfactuals. |
| Fulfillment backlog monitoring | Only after field audit passes | Keep readiness-gated. |
| Meta Ads spend/order monitoring | Only after connector truth gate passes | Growth expansion, not default pilot. |
| Autonomous actions | No | Human decides in the pilot. |
| Fiscal/accounting certainty | No | Readiness only until source/permission gates exist. |

## Pilot success scorecard

Use this scorecard on days 7, 14, 21, and 25-28.

| Dimension | Pass signal | Weak signal |
|---|---|---|
| Case value | At least 1 useful case with evidence | Only empty briefs or noisy alerts. |
| Data honesty | Orvo can say what is fresh, stale, or limited | Buyer expects certainty despite bad data. |
| Operator habit | Named operator reads/forwards/replies | Owner/operator does not engage. |
| Manual-check replacement | At least one daily check replaced or shortened | “Lo sigo mirando igual todo.” |
| Source trust | Tiendanube stock/orders usable or limits known | Source-of-truth remains unclear. |
| Commercial fit | Buyer can decide on Starter/Growth | Needs procurement, custom scope, or free tier. |
| Scope discipline | Buyer accepts “detects/recommends; human decides” | Buyer demands autonomous actions now. |

### Minimum pilot pass by day 25-28

A pilot is commercially usable if it clears **at least 3 of 5** core checks:

1. one useful case with evidence;
2. one data-health or freshness finding;
3. named operator habit;
4. manual-check time saved or follow-up clarified;
5. clear Starter/Growth/stop recommendation.

If it clears fewer than 3, do not force conversion. Classify the reason: no urgency, no champion, data too dirty, scope mismatch, or product truth gate not ready.

## Discovery close script

### Use after the five-question qualification script

```text
Por lo que me contás, el piloto tiene sentido si lo usamos para validar valor real,
no para venderte otro reporte.

En 30 días, Orvo mira una Tiendanube y te muestra:
1. qué necesita atención hoy,
2. con qué evidencia,
3. qué dato está fresco o stale,
4. qué queda abierto y quién lo sigue.

El precio es USD 149 por 30 días. Si no aparece al menos un caso útil con evidencia,
o si el dato no sirve y eso mismo te ayuda a ordenar la operación, no seguimos.
Si sí aparece valor, al día 25-28 decidimos si pasás a Starter, Growth o si conviene
pausar para limpiar datos.
```

### Close-or-nurture rule

Offer the pilot only if all hard gates pass:

| Gate | Pass signal |
|---|---|
| One Tiendanube store | One store is the pilot control plane. |
| Named pain | Buyer names a recent stockout, stale data, delayed dispatch, sales-floor, or manual-check problem. |
| Named operator | A person will receive and act on cases. |
| Data willingness | Buyer accepts that bad data may limit cases and must be fixed. |
| Paid intent | USD 149 / 30 days or approved ARS equivalent is acceptable. |
| Scope fit | Buyer accepts “Orvo detecta/recomienda; una persona decide.” |

If any gate fails, do not create a custom pilot. Nurture or route to a health-check conversation.

```text
Orvo OS Activation Sprint — USD 149 / 30 días

Para una tienda Tiendanube, Orvo arma un centro operativo inicial:
qué necesita atención hoy, por qué, con qué evidencia, qué queda abierto

Incluye:

No incluye:
- emisión de facturas, asesoramiento fiscal, conciliación contable o movimiento de dinero;
- acciones automáticas sobre Tiendanube, stock, campañas, pedidos o clientes;
- WhatsApp inbox/chatbot para clientes;

- garantía de aumento de ventas.

Criterio de éxito:
Orvo abre o actualiza casos útiles con evidencia, o demuestra honestamente qué dato/fuente
no es confiable. El brief debe reemplazar al menos un chequeo manual o dejar claro
qué seguimiento se estaba perdiendo.
```

## Weekly value proof note

Send this after week 1 and week 3, then use it in the day 25-28 conversion call.

```text
Resumen semanal Orvo — [store]

- Casos abiertos/actualizados: [count]
- Casos útiles según tu feedback: [count]
- Casos de datos stale/no confiables: [count]
- Stock en riesgo detectado: [count]
- Chequeos manuales reemplazados o acortados: [minutes/day × days]
- Falsos positivos/ruido ajustado: [count + adjustment]
- Fuente más confiable: [Tiendanube / shipping / WhatsApp / other]
- Fuente limitada o pendiente: [source + reason]
- Caso más útil de la semana: [case + why]
- Recomendación: [continue / adjust thresholds / fix data / evaluate Growth / pause]

Regla: no decimos “ganaste plata” salvo que tengas evidencia de una acción concreta.
Decimos “revenue at risk”, “tiempo recuperado”, “seguimiento aclarado” o “dato no confiable”.
```

## ROI model for the close call

Use only buyer-provided numbers. If unknown, leave blank.

### A. Manual checking time

```text
Monthly time value = (minutes/day ÷ 60) × 22 × USD/hour
```

Sales line:

> “Si Orvo te ahorra 30 minutos diarios de chequear Tiendanube, WhatsApp, stock y envíos, el piloto ya se acerca a pagarse solo antes de contar stockouts o pedidos trabados.”

### B. Stockout / oversell risk

```text
Revenue at risk = stockouts × missed orders × average order value
```

Use “revenue at risk”, not “saved revenue”, unless the merchant can show the action Orvo triggered.

Sales line:

> “No prometo recuperar ventas. El piloto mide si detectamos a tiempo productos que se están quedando sin stock y si eso te ayuda a actuar antes.”

### C. Fulfillment / WISMO risk

Use only if Tiendanube payment/shipping fields are reliable for this store.

```text
Orders at risk = paid orders aging beyond SLA × claim/refund rate
Support load = WISMO messages × support minutes ÷ 60 × USD/hour
```

Sales line:

> “Si el dato de despacho es confiable, Orvo puede mostrar pedidos pagos esperando. Si no es confiable, abre dato stale en vez de inventar.”

### D. Ad spend without orders

Treat as Growth unless Meta Ads connector and freshness parity are verified.

```text
Spend at risk = daily ad spend × bad-spend hours/days before detection
```

Sales line:

> “No vendemos atribución perfecta. El caso operativo es simple: si hay gasto y no entran pedidos, alguien tiene que mirar rápido.”

## Objection handling

### “USD 149 es mucho para una app de Tiendanube”

```text
Orvo no es una app más de Tiendanube. No compite con Alerti, Envia, Dux o Producteca.
Se ubica arriba: coordina lo que pasa entre Tiendanube, stock, envíos, WhatsApp y datos.
Si hoy pagás varias apps o dedicás 30 minutos diarios a chequearlas, el piloto mide si
Orvo reemplaza ese chequeo y abre casos útiles con evidencia.
```

### “¿Y si no aparecen casos?”

```text
Entonces el piloto también tiene valor: nos dice si el dato no sirve, si falta umbral,
si no hay urgencia operativa o si todavía no hay volumen para Orvo. No tiene sentido
seguir pagando si no aparece al menos un caso útil o un hallazgo claro de datos.
```

### “Ya tengo dashboards”

```text
El dashboard te muestra métricas. Orvo te muestra qué está abierto, qué está stale,
qué evidencia lo sostiene y quién lo sigue. La diferencia es la cola operativa, no otro gráfico.
```

### “Ya tengo Alerti / stock alerts”

```text
Perfecto. Alerti mira stock. Orvo mira si ese stock está fresco, si se cruza con pedidos,
si el dato viene de Tiendanube, si hay seguimiento y si el caso se repite o se resuelve.
No reemplazamos la alerta; coordinamos lo que pasa después.
```

### “Ya tengo WhatsApp / Zoko / WATI”

```text
WhatsApp sigue siendo el canal. Orvo no es inbox ni chatbot. Orvo manda al WhatsApp
una proyección breve del estado operativo: casos abiertos, evidencia, stale data y próximos pasos.
El historial queda en Orvo para que no se pierda en mensajes.
```

### “Quiero que pause campañas / cambie stock / emita facturas”

```text
Eso no entra en el primer Sprint. Orvo detecta y recomienda; una persona decide.
Si más adelante una acción tiene caso, evidencia, permisos, auditoría y rollback, se puede
evaluar. Hoy vendemos control operativo, no acciones automáticas ni asesoramiento fiscal.
```

### “¿Podés decirme qué falta facturar?”

```text
En el Sprint no emitimos facturas ni damos asesoramiento fiscal. Podemos mapear qué
fuente usan para facturación y marcar si falta conectar una fuente confiable. Hasta que
eso exista, Orvo no inventa faltantes.
```

### “¿Puede conciliar Mercado Pago o banco?”

```text
No en el primer Sprint. Sí podemos mapear qué fuente usan para cobros y qué sería
necesario para monitorear excepciones después. Orvo no mueve dinero ni reconcilia libros.
```

## Qualification overlay: companion stack maturity

Use this as a fast tie-breaker when several leads look similar.

| Companion stack signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Tiendanube + ERP-lite + shipping + WhatsApp + manual admin | Strong pilot fit: real complexity plus resolver capacity. |
| Tiendanube + agency/freelancer + Meta Ads + WhatsApp | Strong if owner remains buyer and agency can act on cases. |
| Tiendanube + Producteca/BaseLinker sync | Strong Growth signal; require source-of-truth discipline. |
| Tiendanube + only one cheap alert app | Weak price anchor; likely to compare against USD 7-30 apps. |
| Heavy stack but no one can name source of truth | Require data-health check before live monitoring. |
| Marketplace-first with Tiendanube peripheral | Deprioritize unless Tiendanube is still the D2C source. |

Best early buyer:

> Tiendanube merchant with 3+ operational tools, a named owner/operator, WhatsApp-first routine, and at least one recent operational miss.

## Conversion decision tree

### Convert to Starter when

- one Tiendanube store is the right unit;
- daily brief plus basic case lifecycle is enough;
- `data_stale`, mapped `stockout_risk`, and conservative sales-floor cases are valuable;
- support load is trending toward repeatable SaaS;
- at least one useful case or data-health finding created a clear operational action.

Close line:

> “El piloto validó que Orvo te sirve como desk diario para una tienda. Mi recomendación es pasar a Starter: mismo brief, mismos casos base, menos concierge.”

### Convert to Growth when

- the buyer asks for more recipients, workflow depth, weekly review, higher limits, or agency accountability;
- fulfillment backlog is important and passed the truth gate;
- Meta Ads monitoring is the strongest ROI path after connector readiness;
- ongoing human review is desired and should be priced.

Close line:

> “Lo que pedís ya no es solo un brief diario: es workflow operativo. Eso corresponde a Growth porque incluye más seguimiento, más límites y revisión semanal.”

### Stop or pause when

- data hygiene is poor and the buyer will not fix it;
- most days are empty because the store lacks operational urgency;
- the buyer wants dashboards, chatbot flows, or autonomous external actions instead of cases;
- no named operator reads or acts on the brief;
- concierge time exceeds willingness to pay and produces no reusable learning.

Pause line:

> “Hoy no conviene seguir pagando. Primero hay que resolver [datos/champion/volumen/alcance]. Te propongo revisar en 60-90 días o cuando cambie [trigger].”
